Clip Editing

Intentional pacing without noise or gimmicks

Choose your editing intensity before generation or when re-making any clip. Sound accents stay independently controllable, and quiet or uniform moments are never forced into arbitrary motion.

Off

Untouched

Keeps original framing, color, and audio completely steady — ideal when you only want clean 9:16 reframing and captions.

  • Zero zoom motion or color shifts
  • Loudness-normalized voice only
  • Explicitly marked as unedited

Balanced

Default

Calm, editorial polish designed for speech-heavy clips, commentary, and gameplay moments.

  • Smooth opening arrival settle & clarity grade
  • Timed setup → payoff → reaction punch-ins with natural holds
  • Audible, restrained synthesized sound accents (separate toggle)

Energetic

High pace

Tighter pacing and expressive reframing built for fast reactions, clutches, and highlight reels.

  • Snappier opening arrival & crisper tonal contrast
  • Closer reframing shifts & punchier holds on turning points
  • Full facecam protection on gameplay layouts

FAQ

Common questions

How do I re-make an older clip with the new editing presets?

Open any video or creator clip in the studio, click Edit & Re-make, choose Off, Balanced, or Energetic, toggle Sound accents independently if you want, and press Save and re-make. Your clip re-renders cleanly and shows the updated applied-effects report.

Does Anhy Clips force effects onto quiet or short clips?

No. If a clip is under 3 seconds, set to Off, or uniformly quiet with no speech, audio peaks, or visual cuts, Anhy Clips keeps the video steady and explicitly shows why no effects were applied.

What happens when captions are turned off?

Turning captions off hides on-screen subtitles while still using the saved transcript and audio energy signals to time punch-ins and sound accents cleanly.

Are the sound accents copyright-safe?

Yes. Every whoosh, low-end accent, and tactile pop is synthesized on the fly inside FFmpeg — zero third-party stock packs and zero copyright claims.

How does facecam framing work on gaming streams?

Choose Gameplay to fill the clip with the game and keep the webcam in a protected corner, or Streamer + gameplay to put the streamer in the top half and gameplay in the bottom half. Anhy Clips detects the webcam automatically; if it cannot find one, it uses full-screen gameplay.
